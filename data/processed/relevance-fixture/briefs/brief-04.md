# Client brief 04 - mid-size manufacturer of packaging

The finance director set a target to lower operating costs next year and asked which routine processes can be automated. The company noticed that its accounting and planning software now ships with built-in AI assistants that need no separate project. The plant engineers tried a no-code tool in October and built a working demand forecast within two weeks. Two recent hires already have data and automation skills, which was not the case a year ago.

A key supplier of cardboard raised prices in September. The company sponsors the local football club.
