# Client brief 09 - publisher of primary school teaching material

The publisher is preparing lesson material about AI for pupils aged ten to twelve. Teachers who tested the draft in September said some explanations were too complicated for that age. They asked for classroom activities in which children discuss, ask their own questions and explore ideas together. The editors want to present both the advantages and the dangers without frightening the children.

The publisher changed its printing house in August. Paper costs rose during the quarter.
