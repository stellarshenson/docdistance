# Client brief 07 - advisory board of a holding company

The board will not act on survey findings until it understands how the survey was carried out. In October it asked who the respondents were, what roles they hold and how they were selected. It wants to know which countries were covered and how large the sample was. One member asked how precise the published percentages are.

The holding company sold its property division in August. Its annual meeting is planned for March.
