# Client brief 02 - insurance claims back office

In the last quarter the claims department lost several experienced handlers and could not find replacements with the needed digital skills. Management wants software to take over the repetitive data entry so that the remaining staff can deal with complex cases. A first self-service portal for policyholders went live in September and already answers routine questions. The works council asked for a training programme so that employees learn to work with the new automation tools.

The company also renewed its office lease for five years. Storm damage in the north raised claim volumes in July.
