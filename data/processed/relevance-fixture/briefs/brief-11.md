# Client brief 11 - advertising agency

The agency's designers fear that image and text generators will replace creative work. The creative director argued in October that the tools only recombine what they have already seen and cannot produce a truly original idea. Account managers pointed out that clients value a person who understands their feelings and responds with empathy. The agency wants a balanced view of what such tools can and cannot do before it changes its staffing plan.

It won a regional industry award in September. The office kitchen was renovated over the summer.
