# Client brief 10 - recruitment agency

In September the agency found that its candidate ranking tool favoured applicants who resemble earlier hires. The tool had learned from ten years of past placement decisions made by people. The managing director does not believe the supplier intended to discriminate, but wants the unfair pattern in the historic records corrected before the tool is used again. A diversity review of the whole selection process starts in November.

The agency opened an office in a second city. Two large clients extended their contracts.
