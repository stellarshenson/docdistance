# Client brief 03 - consumer lender

A regulator asked the lender in September to show how its credit scoring model reaches each decision. Customer complaints mention that rejected applicants receive no reason they can understand. An internal audit found that nobody records where the training data came from. The board requested a written policy on the ethical use of automated decisions before the end of the year, and wants to know whether the model treats applicant groups differently.

The lender moved its call centre to a new provider in August. Interest margins narrowed slightly over the quarter.
