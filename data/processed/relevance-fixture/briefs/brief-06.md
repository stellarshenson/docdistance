# Client brief 06 - large bank with operations in Spain and India

The executive committee asked in September how the bank's use of AI compares with other large organisations in its sector. It has several models in production, while its Spanish unit is still only experimenting. Over the past two years the group increased its spending on AI and brought forward several deployments, and it wants to know whether competitors in other countries moved faster. The committee also asked how many peers are already working with generative AI.

The bank closed forty branches in the last quarter. A new chief risk officer joined in August.
