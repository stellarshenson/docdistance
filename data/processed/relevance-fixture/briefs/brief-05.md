# Client brief 05 - online electronics retailer

The retailer is choosing where to apply AI first and has drawn up a list of candidate areas. Its IT team wants to automate server maintenance and to detect intrusion attempts earlier. The sales department asked for help with marketing campaigns and with spotting fraudulent orders. Customer service proposed a self-service assistant, and the finance team wants support for planning and for the supply chain.

The retailer opened a second distribution centre in July. Return rates rose after a change of courier.
