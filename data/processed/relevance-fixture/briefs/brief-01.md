# Client brief 01 - regional logistics operator

Over the last three months the company paused two machine learning pilots because nobody on the team could maintain the models after the contractor left. Its customer records sit in four separate systems with conflicting formats, and the cleanup work was larger than planned. The legal counsel raised concerns that staff may paste confidential shipment data into public chatbots. Quotes from three vendors came in well above the approved budget for the year.

In August the company opened a new warehouse near the port. The fleet renewal programme was moved to the next financial year.
