# Client brief 12 - consumer association

Members of the association asked how much of their daily life is already steered by algorithms without their knowledge. Examples raised in the last quarter were film suggestions, unlocking a phone with a face scan, voice assistants and route planning. The association wants to explain in plain words how such systems learn from examples and why they need large amounts of data. It also plans to warn members that every click and purchase leaves data that later shapes what they are shown.

The association elected a new chairperson in September. Membership fees stay unchanged next year.
