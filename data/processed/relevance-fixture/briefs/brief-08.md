# Client brief 08 - private hospital group

The group stopped a trial of a generative AI assistant in September because doctors do not trust answers they cannot verify and patient data might leave the organisation. The project team lacked people who know how to implement such a system. The ethics committee asked for a formal policy on AI and for evidence that the tools do not disadvantage patient groups. Patients told a survey that they prefer providers who are open about how automated decisions are made.

The group opened a new clinic in October. The cafeteria contract was put out to tender.
