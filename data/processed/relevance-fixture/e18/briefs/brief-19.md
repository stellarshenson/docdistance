# Client brief 19 - software startup

The startup built a working prototype of an AI feature in the spring, but connecting it to the existing product and running it for all customers has proven much harder than expected. The developers complain that they lack proper tooling for building and testing models. The price of the computing capacity needed is beyond what the company can pay this year. The founders also worry that their small team does not have enough machine learning knowledge.

A second funding round closed in September. The company hired a new head of sales. Its office moved to a shared workspace.
