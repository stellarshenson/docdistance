# Client brief 18 - procurement team of a utility company

- The team is assessing a large technology vendor as a long-term partner for cloud infrastructure and AI
- It wants to know in how many countries the vendor serves clients and whether public bodies and operators of critical infrastructure are among them
- A further question is whether the vendor also offers consulting, not only software
- The utility hopes to simplify its business processes and lower costs with the vendor's platform
- The tender for new smart meters closes in November
