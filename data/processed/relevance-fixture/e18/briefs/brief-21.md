# Client brief 21 - teacher training college

The college is designing a course that prepares future primary school teachers to talk about AI with their pupils. Trainee teachers reported in October that they do not know how much detail children of that age can follow. The lecturers want the course to show both what AI makes possible and where its risks lie. They favour lessons built on open discussion, in which every child can speak and ideas are explored through questions. The course should leave out consequences that children of that age cannot yet grasp.

The college received a state grant in September.
