# Client brief 24 - regional data protection office

The office is preparing a public information campaign about the data traces people leave behind. It wants to explain that almost everything done on a phone or a computer produces data that can be analysed later. Citizens asked how streaming services and navigation apps decide what to suggest. The campaign will also cover the risk that systems trained on records of past human decisions repeat old unfairness. The office wants to stress that the quality of an automated decision depends on the quality of the data behind it.

A new commissioner took office in October.
