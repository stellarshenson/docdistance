# Client brief 17 - hotel chain

The chain plans its first AI projects for the coming year. Candidates are an assistant that answers guest questions, automatic processing of invoices and booking documents, and better analysis of occupancy data. The IT manager also wants to automate routine maintenance of the reservation servers. The projects became realistic because the booking software the chain already uses now includes AI functions. Pressure to cut costs in the back office is the main reason for the plan. A consultant showed that current tools can be set up without a large specialist team.

Two hotels were renovated over the summer. The chain signed a partnership with a regional airline.
