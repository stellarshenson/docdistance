# Client brief 22 - city council social services

- An audit in September showed that the council's risk model flags residents of two districts far more often than others
- The model was trained on case files from the last fifteen years, which reflect how inspectors chose whom to check
- The council does not think the software was built to discriminate, but it sees that old unfair practice lives on in the records
- Before the model is used again the council wants safeguards and a correction of the historic records
- The council opened a new advice centre in October
- The budget for the coming year was approved
