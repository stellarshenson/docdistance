# Client brief 23 - operator of care homes

The operator is considering companion robots and an AI program that composes music for its residents. Care staff doubt that a machine can show real compassion. A pilot in September showed that the robot recognises when a resident looks sad, but residents said it does not feel what they feel. The music therapist argued that the program only varies pieces it has heard before and cannot create something truly new. The managers accept that the technology outperforms people in some tasks, such as predicting falls. They want a sober view of its limits before deciding. The operator also wants to discuss the advantages and the risks with the families.

A new home opened in the spring. Energy costs fell after a renovation. The annual inspection passed without remarks.
