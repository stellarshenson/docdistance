# Client brief 15 - industrial equipment group

Over the last two years the group has brought forward several AI projects and raised their budgets. The board asked in October whether companies in other countries are speeding up in the same way. Most of the new money went into research and development and into retraining employees. The group wants to know which of these two investment areas other firms give priority to.

A strike at a supplier delayed deliveries in August. The group refinanced a loan in September.
